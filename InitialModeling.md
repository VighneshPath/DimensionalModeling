Problem Extension — Global Location Modeling

In addition to borrowing analytics, the business wants to analyze performance across geographical locations of library branches (stores).
Library branches operate across multiple countries, and business users frequently analyze metrics such as revenue, fines, and borrow activity by geographical rollups.
The business wants the ability to analyze data across the following hierarchy levels:

Postal Code
City
State / Province
Country
Typical dashboards include rollups such as:
Revenue by City
Revenue by State / Province
Revenue by Country
Borrow trends by Postal Code
Sample Business Questions:
The analytics team wants to answer questions such as:
What is the total revenue generated by city?
Which states generate the most rental revenue?
What are the top countries by borrow volume?
Which postal codes have the highest circulation of books?
How does revenue trend across regions over time?

These questions require the ability to aggregate and roll up location hierarchies.

Location Data Challenges:
Branch location data coming from operational systems may be incomplete or inconsistent. For example:

Postal codes may be missing
State names may vary in format
Cities may have duplicate spellings
To standardize geographical data, assume that the location information is cleaned and enriched using the GeoNames dataset.
GeoNames provides standardized global geographical data including:

Postal codes
Cities
Administrative regions (states / provinces)
Countries
Reference dataset:
https://download.geonames.org/export/dump/
Assume that the store location data has been cleaned and mapped to GeoNames identifiers before entering the analytics layer.

Countries in Scope for This Exercise
For this assignment, assume library branches exist in the following countries:

India (IN)
Australia (AU)
Spain (ES)
France (FR)


These countries are chosen because they represent different geographical hierarchy structures.
Modeling Challenge
Your task is to think about how this global location hierarchy should be represented in the dimensional model so that the business questions above can be answered.

1. If it's within dim branch
